# TikTok creative patterns

Use this reference when the opening, narrative format, sharing mechanism, or repeatable series structure is unclear.

## Hook patterns

- **Unexpected shortcut:** "I found a much easier way to..."
- **Hidden truth:** "Nobody tells you this about..."
- **Costly experiment:** "I spent [amount or time] testing these, so you do not have to."
- **Delayed usefulness:** "This looks useless until you see this."
- **Corrective tension:** "You are probably doing this wrong."
- **Result first:** Show the outcome, then explain how it happened.
- **Mid-action:** Begin inside the action, decision, or conflict.
- **Specific consequence:** "This one [mistake] caused [measurable result]."

Select a hook for its fit with the evidence and payoff. Treat dramatic wording as useful only when it is accurate.

## Repeatable formats

- **Contrarian explanation:** State the common advice, challenge it, then prove why.
- **Transformation:** Show before, the decisive process, then after.
- **Experiment:** State what was tested, reveal the result, then explain the lesson.
- **Ranking:** Compare a bounded set using explicit criteria and reveal the winners.
- **Problem and fix:** Demonstrate the symptom, identify the cause, then show the fix.
- **Open-loop story:** Start at the consequential moment, escalate, then resolve it.
- **Reply video:** Use a real comment as immediate context, then answer or demonstrate.

Favor two or three formats that match the creator's strengths and can recur with new subjects.

## Transmission mechanisms

Map the premise to one primary audience reaction:

- **"That is me."** Identity or relatability.
- **"That is you."** A precise observation someone sends to a friend.
- **"I did not know that."** Novel information with credible proof.
- **"I need this later."** A practical template, checklist, or technique.
- **"No way."** Surprise, spectacle, or transformation.
- **"I disagree."** A defensible choice with real tradeoffs.
- **"Finally, someone said it."** A clear articulation of an existing feeling.
- **"Look at this."** A result that is intrinsically worth showing another person.

## Natural closes

Match the close to the intended response:

- Save: "Save this before your next [specific situation]."
- Share: End on the relatable line or reveal when it carries the sharing reason by itself.
- Comment: Ask for a genuine choice, experience, or counterexample.
- Rewatch: End on a detail that makes the opening newly meaningful.
- Continue: Tease the next distinct payoff only when the next installment is planned.

Let the payoff end the video when it is the strongest possible close.
